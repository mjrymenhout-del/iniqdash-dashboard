# IniqDash name, identity and attribution

The **code** of IniqDash is free software under the [Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE). This page is about something separate: the
**name** of the official project and how to tell it apart from other versions. Nothing here limits what MPL-2.0 lets you do with the code
— you may use, study, change and redistribute it, commercially or not, and you may fork it.

## The official project
- **Name:** IniqDash — one word, capital *I* and capital *D*.
- **Official repository:** [`mjrymenhout-del/iniqdash-dashboard`](https://github.com/mjrymenhout-del/iniqdash-dashboard) on GitHub. Official releases are the
  ones published there by its maintainers.
- **Original creator and founder:** Marcel Rymenhout, who founded and originally developed IniqDash in 2026.
- **What it is:** a web-based frontend for Home Assistant. *Home Assistant is the backend. IniqDash is the frontend.* IniqDash is not
  affiliated with or endorsed by Home Assistant / the Open Home Foundation, and it is a different project from IniqNode Studio.

## Logo and visual identity
There is **no official IniqDash logo yet** (the sign-in page shows a generic house icon). When one is created, the founder decides who
owns it and under which terms it may be used; until then no logo should be presented as "the IniqDash logo". The application's visual
design (themes, widgets) is part of the code and is covered by MPL-2.0 like the rest of it.

## Trademark status
"IniqDash" is **not a registered trademark** as far as this repository records. The project uses the name to identify the official
project; this page asks for fair use of it and does not claim registered rights.

## Referring to the project — always fine
- Saying that something works with, is built on, is a fork of, or is compatible with IniqDash, e.g. "Based on IniqDash by Marcel Rymenhout".
- Linking to the official repository, writing articles, tutorials and reviews, showing screenshots.
- Keeping the original copyright and licence notices (MPL-2.0 requires that for its files).

## Forks and modified versions
You are free to fork IniqDash under MPL-2.0. To avoid confusing users:
- Use a **different name** for a distributed fork (e.g. "*YourName* Dashboard — based on IniqDash"), or make clear in its name and README
  that it is an **unofficial** version.
- Do not present a fork, a modified build, a hosted service or a package as the official IniqDash release, and do not imply endorsement by
  the IniqDash project or its founder.
- Keep the attribution: the copyright notices in the files and a line in your README naming IniqDash and its founder.

These are requests for honest identification, not restrictions on the licence's rights. They also protect users: security fixes and
support questions should reach the right project.

## Asking for permission
To use the IniqDash name for something that could look official (a package in a store, a hosted service, an event, merchandise), or
to use a future official logo, open an issue in the official repository titled "Branding request" describing the use, or contact the
founder through the contact details on the repository. Permission is given in writing (an issue reply by a maintainer is enough).

## Contributors
Contributors keep the copyright in their contributions and license them under MPL-2.0 ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). They are
credited in the Git history and the changelog; the founder's role as original creator stays recorded in the README and here.
