## Not third-party (for the avoidance of doubt)
- Apart from the photographs above, every image shipped with IniqDash is **original project artwork** under the project licence (MPL-2.0): the theme backgrounds
  (`assets/themes/*.svg`), the room and widget sample art (`*.svg`), and the raster samples (`assets/img/media/artwork-*.jpg`,
  `widgets/default/*/assets/*.png`, `widgets/default/person/assets/avatar-demo.jpg`). The raster samples are rendered from the SVG sources in
  `tools/artwork/` (`node tools/artwork/render.mjs`); the earlier images of unrecorded origin were replaced by these on 2026-11-12, and the
  two oldest SVG samples (`camera/assets/demo-garden.svg`, `image/assets/demo-house.svg`) were redrawn the same day.
- The scene and demo-camera pictures (`assets/img/scenes/*.jpg`, `assets/img/cameras/*.jpg`) are original illustrations drawn by
  `tools/artwork/scenes.mjs` (no photographs, no third-party designs); the cameras are labelled as demo images wherever they are shown.
- The documentation screenshots (`docs/`) show IniqDash itself with its simulated demo data.
- Home Assistant is a separate project (Apache-2.0) and is **not** included; IniqDash talks to it over its public API.
